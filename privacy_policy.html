Privacy Policy - Water Sort Master

Last updated: November 2026

Water Sort Master ("the app") is a puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app uses.

Data we collect

  • Game progress (level, coins, settings) is stored on your device and in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under an anonymous ID, so you can restore progress.
  • Analytics and crash data (events such as level start/complete, device model, OS version) via Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, to improve the game.
  • Advertising: the app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID and similar data to serve and measure ads. In the EEA/UK you are asked for consent, and you can change it in Settings > Privacy Settings.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

Data sharing

Data is shared only with the service providers above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, Google Play). We do not sell personal data.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

You can delete local data from Settings > Delete My Data, change ad consent in Privacy Settings, or reset your advertising ID in Android settings. To request deletion of cloud data, contact us at the email below.
